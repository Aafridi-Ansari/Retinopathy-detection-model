Mathematical Traceability

Translating raw ocular pixels into verifiable clinical diagnostic decisions through deep gradient attribution

Loss: Categorical Cross-Entropy • Backprop: Grad-CAM++
Stage 01

Retinal Fundus Frame

Dim: (512, 512, 3)
Stage 02

Conv5 Feature Maps

Deep convolutional layers extract high-level representations of microaneurysms, hemorrhages, and lipid exudates.

Conv5: (16, 16, 2048)
Stage 03

Gradient Attribution

Computes partial derivative of target class score with respect to feature maps: α_k = 1/Z ∑(∂y^c / ∂A^k)

Grad-CAM Heatmap

Linear combination and ReLU activation localizes focal lesion clusters: L^c = ReLU(∑ α_k A^k)

Saliency: (500, 500)

Problem Statement & Hackathon Solution

Explainable AI for Diabetic Retinopathy Screening in Rural India

India has over 77 million diabetic individuals. In rural areas, the ratio of ophthalmologists is less than 1 per 250,000 citizens. Because early Diabetic Retinopathy is completely asymptomatic, over 80% of affected rural patients only seek medical attention when irreversible blindness has already occurred.

Zero Early Symptoms

Patients feel zero pain while microvascular capillary breakdown silently progresses to retinal detachment.

Smartphone Telescreening

Equipping rural ASHA workers with low-cost condensing lenses to capture fundus scans at primary health sub-centers.

Trust through Grad-CAM XAI

Providing explainable heatmaps so rural healthcare providers and patients clearly understand WHY the AI flagged the condition.

International Clinical Diabetic Retinopathy (ICDR) Grading Scale

Stage Diagnosis Key Pathological Biomarkers Urgency
Grade 0 No DR No microaneurysms or retinal abnormalities Annual Follow-up
Grade 1 Mild NPDR Microaneurysms only (isolated focal ballooning) 6-9 Months
Grade 2 Moderate NPDR More than microaneurysms: blot hemorrhages, hard exudates 2-4 Weeks Specialist
Grade 3 Severe NPDR 4-2-1 rule: hemorrhages in 4 quadrants, venous beading, IRMA Urgent (48-72h)
Grade 4 Proliferative DR (PDR) Neovascularization, preretinal/vitreous hemorrhage Emergency Same-Day
